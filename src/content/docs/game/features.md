Development runs in milestones. This list shows what exists today.

## People

- Needs, personalities and free will (autonomy)
- Social life: conversations, relationships, neighbours and visitors
- Day and night, jobs, careers with promotions, and job hunting
- Skills and long-term ambitions
- Service visitors

## Building

- Buy mode with a catalog of objects
- Build mode: walls (including diagonal ones), multiple storeys, windows you can see through
- Roofs, built automatically or by hand
- Terraforming, water, ponds and docks

## World

- Gardens and growing plants
- Neighbourhoods and a generated town with roads, crossings and different settlement types
- Seasons, weather and light
- Grass, a painted ground surface (gravel, paths, flagstones, soil, sand…) and procedural trees

## Saving

- Save and load the whole world

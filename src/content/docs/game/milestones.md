Project Vita is built in milestones. Each one has a plan, gets built step by step, and ends
with a manual playtest. This page lists the milestones in broad strokes.

## The MVP (October 2026)

| Milestone | Date | What was built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M0** Project setup | 9 Oct | Godot project, folder layout, test runner and editor tooling. |
| **M1** World & camera | 9 Oct | A flat lot with a grid, an isometric camera with pan, rotate and zoom, and tile picking. |
| **M2** Simulation core | 9 Oct | A deterministic simulation with a clock, needs, smart objects, room-based route finding and utility AI that picks what to do next. Fully unit-tested and independent of graphics. |
| **M3** Live mode | 9 Oct | People walk around and use objects. The HUD shows needs, need arrows, thought bubbles and alerts. |
| **M4** Buy mode | 9 Oct | An object catalog, money, and buying, moving and selling furniture. |
| **M5** Build mode | 9 Oct | Walls, wall coverings, floors, fences, doors and windows, undo and redo, and a cutaway view. |
| **M6** Multiple storeys | 9 Oct | Floors, stairs, columns, balconies and walking between levels. |
| **M7** Social life | 9 Oct | Conversations about shared interests, jokes, compliments and arguments, and relationships. |
| **M8** Day, night & jobs | 9 Oct | A day–night light cycle, lamps, work schedules, salaries and cooking. |
| **M9** Saving & polish | 9 Oct | Save slots, creating new people, the household bar and object info panels. |
| **M10** Stabilization | 10 Oct | Long automated test runs, balancing, sitting down to eat and the first full playtest. |

## A richer life

| Milestone | Date | What was built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M11** Skills & careers | 10 Oct | Skills learned from books, TV and computers, ten-level careers with promotions and firings, and job hunting. |
| **M12** Neighbours & visitors | 11 Oct | People walk by on the street, ring the doorbell and come in for a visit. |
| **M13** Activity feedback | 11 Oct | You can see at a glance what someone is doing, how long it takes and what they are learning. |
| **M14** Service visitors | 12 Oct | Cleaners, repairers, gardeners and the mail carrier. Things break, and bills arrive in the mail. |

## From one lot to a whole town

| Milestone | Date | What was built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M20** Neighbourhood | 25 Oct | Several lots and households, switching between them, community lots (cafe, market, shop) and the trolley. |
| **M21** Town generator | 27 Oct | Generated towns with roads, crossings, different settlement types and the countryside around them. |
| **M22** Roofs | 8 Nov | Houses get automatic roofs, with style and colour chosen in Build mode. Walls and fences can also run diagonally. |
| **M23** Roof building | 8 Nov | Building roofs by hand from pieces in different styles. |
| **M24** Lots on the map | 8 Nov | The neighbourhood map shows each lot as it really is, with its house, roof and yard. |

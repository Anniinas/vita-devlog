Welcome to the Project Vita devlog!

Project Vita is an isometric life-simulation sandbox I'm building in Godot. You build houses,
move people in and watch them live. You can't give them orders: they decide for themselves
based on their needs, personalities, relationships and the objects around them.

## Where things stand

The project started in October 2026. Since then it has gone through almost thirty milestones:

- **People** have needs, free will, jobs, careers, skills and relationships with neighbours.
- **Build and buy modes** cover walls, multiple storeys, roofs, terraforming, water and docks.
- **The world** has grown into a whole generated town with roads and different kinds of
  settlements.
- **Recently** the world has also got seasons and weather, grass, a ground brush for painting
  paths and flowerbeds, and procedural trees.

## What to expect here

I'll write a post when a milestone is finished. Posts will cover what was built and why, and
what turned out harder than expected. The [game section](../../game/overview/) holds a more
stable description of the game itself.

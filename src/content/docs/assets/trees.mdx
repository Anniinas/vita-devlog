Trees are built procedurally in code: the trunk, branches and leaves are generated from each
species' parameters, and there are no modelled meshes. Every species comes in three sizes:
**sapling**, **young** and **mature**. The pictures show them from left to right, all at the same
scale and from the game's camera angle. Choosing a season on one tree switches every tree on the
page.

In autumn each leaf turns and falls at its own time, so a tree empties gradually. In winter the
broadleaves stand bare and the conifers carry snow.

Prices are in Trin, the game's currency.

## Spruce

A cone with one stem to the top and whorls of branches that droop at the tips. Grey-brown, flaky bark and dark needles. Evergreen, and snow settles on its tiers in winter.
